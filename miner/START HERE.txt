SUMO FROG MINER — START HERE  (test coins only, no real money)
==============================================================

1. Copy sumo_config.example.json and rename the copy to sumo_config.json
   (on Windows: right-click the example, Copy, Paste, then rename).

2. Open sumo_config.json in Notepad. Replace YOUR_SERVER with the test
   gateway address you were given. Leave "network" set to "private" unless
   you were told to switch it to "devnet". Save. The miner cannot connect
   until you do this. Never put a real wallet, seed phrase or private key here.

3. Double-click  Mine SUMO.bat
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then
   "Run anyway". It's a plain text script: right-click it and choose Edit
   to see what it does.

Full steps, payout address and withdraw: see HOW TO MINE.txt
